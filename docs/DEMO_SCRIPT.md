## Recording checklist

Use saved evidence only. Show the Courtroom's captured timestamp and synthetic labels. Avoid private passwords, cookies, delegate keys, raw customer records or wallet recovery material. Pause animations when reading hashes. Do not imply that a simulated callback was an actual supplier phone call or that a rule score measures fraud probability. If a wallet warns, stop and show the documented limitation; do not dismiss it.

## Editorial boundaries

- The payment quote excerpt is a neutral document presentation of the **public original reconciliation JSON**, not a newly fetched 402 or a live checkout screen. The public records contain approval timestamps, executed-byte equality and the delivered result. No original wallet extension signing panel was captured or fabricated.
- The FBI site did not render reliably in the capture browser. The actual homepage guidance card and its official FBI link are used instead, as permitted by the storyboard.
- The recording pipeline may reload a public GET once **before interaction** after a stalled chunk load. It never retries a purchase, memory write or private application action; disabled controls are never forced.
- Timed captions derive from the same TTS stream's word boundaries plus exact inserted PCM pauses. The supplied script has 262 whitespace-separated words; it is preserved rather than padded with marketing copy.
- Source and export checksums, actual capture offsets and the deployed source SHA are recorded in ignored `recordings/capture-manifest.json` and `render/export-manifest.json`. Keep the local source recordings for review.
